## The honest answer on CAC

**There is no verified public CAC for Supernova, MySivi, SpeakX or Stimuler in the collected sources.** It cannot be calculated from downloads, employees, a Meta Ad Library view, funding, annual recurring revenue or total paid users.

Correct consumer CAC:

```text
Fully loaded CAC = attributable paid-media + creative/agency + influencer/affiliate commission
                   + allocated growth payroll
                   -------------------------------------------------------------
                         first-time paid subscribers in that same channel/cohort
```

The denominator must be *first-time paid subscribers*, not installs, app opens, trials or total subscribers. Record country, state/city, language, channel, plan, date, attribution window and refund rate. Calculate payback using contribution margin after payment fees, AI inference, support and any teacher/mentor time.

Correct B2B CAC:

```text
Fully loaded B2B CAC = attributable marketing + sales + solution/onboarding labour
                       -------------------------------------------------------------
                              new closed accounts
```

Pair it with sales-cycle length, implementation cost, activated seats, renewal and expansion; an enterprise contract that never activates is not a successful acquisition.

## What the comparisons really say

- **MySivi is not demonstrably “far better” than Supernova on public financial evidence.** Supernova’s reported ARR is higher, but it is a single unnamed-source report; MySivi reports profitability, but without an audited amount.
- MySivi’s AI-plus-peer design is a credible product explanation for engagement/referral potential, but no public data here proves lower CAC or better retention.
- SpeakX has the clearest public paid-scale claim in the set. That makes it a stronger benchmark for price, localized product and paid conversion—not a reason to assume its economics can be copied.
- The valuable competitor research is a dated, local product audit: free gate, price, onboarding language, first speaking success, re-engagement loop, human escalation, checkout and support. Use the separate two-reviewer scorecard to capture it.
