## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** BPO, nearshore and job-transition cohorts in major cities.
- **Most testable outcome:** Bogot�/Barranquilla service simulations and manager reporting.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Institutes, public work-English programmes and consumer apps are substitutes.
- **Main risk:** No evidence supports broad claim that most job postings require English.
- **Evidence signal:** ANIF finds English requirement concentrated in limited role/city sample, while public programmes also add free supply.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Spanish. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (480 in 2025, -5 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
