## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** Young IT outsourcing, BPO/KPO and mobility cohort; partner-led route.
- **Most testable outcome:** Remote-client English for IT support, BPO/KPO and IT-park partners.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Emerging IT training ecosystem; less mature app landscape than large markets.
- **Main risk:** Payment, language, willingness-to-pay and compliance research before launch.
- **Evidence signal:** Official IT-service export series shows rapid computer-software growth; $5bn policy export target is not actual forecast.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Uzbek and Russian. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (429 in 2025, -10 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
