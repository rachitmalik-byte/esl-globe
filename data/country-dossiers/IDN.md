## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** Mobile-first, price-sensitive learners; institutions reduce CAC.
- **Most testable outcome:** Vocational/SME service cohorts with AI speaking and supervisor dashboards.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Mature localized edtech; Bahasa onboarding and light data usage are table stakes.
- **Main risk:** Do not launch generic consumer subscription into crowded market.
- **Evidence signal:** Government Digital Society Index tracks infrastructure, literacy, empowerment and work.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Bahasa Indonesia. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (471 in 2025, +3 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
