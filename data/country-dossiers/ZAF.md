## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** GBS/BPO employers and specialist professionals, not remedial learners.
- **Most testable outcome:** Customer-experience QA, specialist communication and manager-led upskilling.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** High proficiency and developed BPO provision.
- **Main risk:** Weak fit for entry-level ESL; benchmark/high-ACV niche only.
- **Evidence signal:** GBS sector added 8,180 international jobs and R2.3bn export revenue in Apr-Jun 2025.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: English plus local languages. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (602 in 2025, +8 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
