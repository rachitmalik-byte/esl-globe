## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** Large consumer/corporate opportunity; Portuguese depth and distribution required.
- **Most testable outcome:** Employer-paid tech, export or global-services English after core playbook is proven.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Mature schools, tutors and online providers increase CAC/localisation needs.
- **Main risk:** Huge TAM can hide poor unit economics.
- **Evidence signal:** Digital-skill gap persists; HR survey finds English gaps relevant to global services, but neither measures paid demand.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Brazilian Portuguese. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (482 in 2025, +16 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
