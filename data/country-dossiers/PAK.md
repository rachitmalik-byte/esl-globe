## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** IT/ITeS employees, freelancers and export-oriented SMEs.
- **Most testable outcome:** Client-call and business communication for IT/ITeS/freelancers.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** English tuition and freelancer training are widespread; local app snapshot pending.
- **Main risk:** Macro, payments and data-policy risk; local billing and employer pilots first.
- **Evidence signal:** FY25 ICT service exports rose 23.7% to $2.825bn in July-March period; sector proxy.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Urdu plus regional languages. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (493 in 2025, +0 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
