## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** Export BPO/ITO, tourism and professional learners; English is additive to French/Arabic.
- **Most testable outcome:** European/global customer service, tech support and tourism English.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Large outsourcing ecosystem; French-centric training is a material substitute.
- **Main risk:** English-only product narrative will not fit linguistic reality.
- **Evidence signal:** 2023 outsourcing: 1,200+ firms, 141,000 jobs and MAD17.9bn exports; ministry figures.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Arabic and French. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (492 in 2025, +13 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
