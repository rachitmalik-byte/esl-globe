## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** Manufacturing, logistics, tech and customer-service employers; Spanish-first sales/content.
- **Most testable outcome:** Technical/customer English for nearshoring supervisors, support and logistics teams.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Many consumer options; less proven role-specific assessment systems.
- **Main risk:** Long enterprise sales cycles and strong regional variation.
- **Evidence signal:** Nearshoring shows early manufacturing-job effects; 68% of employers report difficulty finding qualified talent in IDB analysis.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Spanish. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (440 in 2025, -19 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
