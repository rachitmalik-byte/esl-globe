## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** Large theoretical demand, gated by access and education-policy constraints.
- **Most testable outcome:** Only through compliant local partner after legal review; no direct-launch recommendation.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Large domestic incumbents and regulated education-technology environment.
- **Main risk:** Regulation, data, content, payments and foreign-provider position are gate conditions.
- **Evidence signal:** 2025 language-law revision reinforces governance of online/public language use; not full edtech legal analysis.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Mandarin Chinese. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (464 in 2025, +9 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
