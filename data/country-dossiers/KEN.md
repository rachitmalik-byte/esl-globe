## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** BPO, KPO, AI-data and technical-support cohorts; basic English is not need.
- **Most testable outcome:** Global-client communication, QA and AI-data/BPO role simulations.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Strong English/mobile-money and growing BPO/AI-data supply.
- **Main risk:** Do not equate BPO growth with beginner ESL demand.
- **Evidence signal:** Investment agency reports 9% GDP ICT contribution, 85% internet penetration and BPO growth.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: English and Swahili. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (593 in 2025, +12 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
