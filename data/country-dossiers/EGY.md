## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** Offshoring employers and job candidates; B2B/B2G beats consumer subscription.
- **Most testable outcome:** Customer/technical-support speaking paths connected to employer assessment.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Training ecosystem growing with outsourcing; Arabic localisation required.
- **Main risk:** Affordability, partner sales cycles and local content.
- **Evidence signal:** Digital offshoring exports doubled from $2.4bn in 2022 to $4.8bn in 2025, per ITIDA.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Arabic. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (458 in 2025, -7 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
