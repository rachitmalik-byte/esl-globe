## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** GBS and professional communication cohorts, not remedial mass market.
- **Most testable outcome:** Client communication, leadership, writing and assessment in GBS.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** High proficiency and mature training reduce basic-English whitespace.
- **Main risk:** Narrow high-ACV niche, not a volume entry market.
- **Evidence signal:** ICT/e-commerce contributed 23.4% of economy in 2024; 749 GBS entities reported in 2025.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Malay and English. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (581 in 2025, +15 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
