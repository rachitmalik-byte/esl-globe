## Commercial reality

- **Likely payer:** Professional, tourism, migration and digital-service learners.
- **Most testable outcome:** Tourism, professional services and export-related partner cohorts.
- **Existing/substitute supply:** Existing education supply makes generic practice weakly differentiated.
- **Main risk:** Scale and B2B channel strength need paid pilot proof.
- **Evidence signal:** Government digitisation and digital-economy activity are rising; not a direct English signal.

## Delivery / localisation assumption

- Begin validation in: Spanish. This is a product-design assumption, not a measure of English demand. Test code-switching, speech recognition, payment preferences and trust language with local users.
- Do not use the EF EPI score (519 in 2025, +0 vs 2024) as a national fluency or willingness-to-pay estimate. The EPI sample is self-selected online test takers.

## First pilot to run

1. Recruit one employer, college, training centre or other payer matching the stated target outcome.
2. Offer a role-specific 4-6 week speaking path in the local onboarding language.
3. Capture baseline and endline roleplay/assessment; instrument activation, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, AI minutes, support and any human escalation.
4. Compare fully loaded CAC and gross margin against a generic consumer-ad acquisition test.
